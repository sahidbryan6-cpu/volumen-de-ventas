# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Delegated (inferred from the brief): a single self-contained `index.html` (inline CSS/JS), Three.js r128 UMD + GSAP 3 from cdnjs, Google Fonts. Data generated with Python and inlined as one JSON object.

## Users
Classmates and the teacher watching a live class presentation, projected on a screen or shown on a laptop; the presenter (the student) advances slides with keyboard, wheel, swipe or buttons.

## Product Purpose
A web slide deck for the course topic "Análisis de Mercado — 2.2.2 Volumen de ventas". Success: the audience understands, in plain Spanish, what sales volume is, how it differs from sales value, how revenue is computed, how it moves over time and by season/product, and how to read it.

## Positioning
One idea per slide, 70% visual / 30% text, max ~15 words per text block, everyday analogies ("cuántas cajas salieron de la tienda").

## Operating Context
Projected in a classroom (often a dim room, low-quality projector), presenter talking over each slide. Possibly also opened later by the teacher on a phone or laptop.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Spanish language only.
- Nine slides in fixed order (portada → conclusión).
- Navigation: arrows, wheel, swipe, buttons, progress bar, counter, F for fullscreen.
- Only CDNs: cdnjs, jsdelivr, Google Fonts.

## Evidence on Hand
No real sales data. All figures are illustrative and must be labeled "datos ilustrativos", kept in one replaceable JSON object.

## Product Principles
- One idea per slide.
- Show the quantity, don't just say it.
- Plain language and everyday examples over jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, prefers-reduced-motion respected.
